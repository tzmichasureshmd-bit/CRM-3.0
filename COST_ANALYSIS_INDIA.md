## 🧠 OpenAI Costs (The AI Brain)

First, let me explain how OpenAI pricing works. It's not a monthly subscription — **you only pay for what you use.**

### How OpenAI Charges:

Think of it like electricity. You pay per "token" (roughly 4 characters of text). When your AI reads or writes text, that's tokens being used.

**The model we use:** GPT-4o-mini (the smart, affordable one)

**Official OpenAI Pricing:**
- Reading text (input): $0.15 per million tokens = ₹12.45
- Writing text (output): $0.60 per million tokens = ₹49.80

## 💡 How to Pay Even Less

Want to cut costs? Here's what actually works:

### 1. Reduce AI Calls
**Already built-in:**
- If OpenAI is down, system uses smart fallbacks (free)
- Lead scoring caches results (don't re-score same lead)
- Reply suggestions only run when you click the button

**You could add:**
- Rate limits per user (max 50 AI calls/day)
- Cache similar questions ("What's my pipeline?" asked twice = 1 AI call)

**Savings:** 30-50% less on OpenAI (₹130-220/month saved)

---

### 2. Stay on Free Tiers Longer
**Tips:**
- Clean up old test data regularly
- Delete duplicate contacts
- Archive old conversations after 6 months
- Use Supabase storage for files (don't use external services)

**Savings:** Stay free for 6+ months (₹2,075/month saved)

---

### 3. Optimize Your Code
**Easy wins:**
- Enable image compression (Vercel setting)
- Use built-in caching (already enabled)
- Minify JavaScript bundles (already done)

**Savings:** Stay on Vercel Free (₹1,660/month saved)
